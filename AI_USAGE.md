# Use of Super Intelligence (SI) / Artificial Intelligence

## How AI supported development

Generative AI was used as a development assistant in building PyroSync. OpenAI Codex assisted with writing and reviewing code, debugging, designing tests, checking integration, and drafting documentation, including the project summary and this disclosure. AI assistance also supported interface wording and the development of scripts used to generate reproducible demonstration fixtures.

AI output was treated as a draft to be evaluated, not as scientific evidence. Software changes were checked through Rust and TypeScript tests, type checking, linting, production builds, and checks against real satellite archive data. The project team remains responsible for the submitted work, its interpretation, and the accuracy of its claims.

## Scientific observations and demonstration data

PyroSync's scientific inputs are satellite observations obtained from NASA FIRMS, not observations invented by a generative model. Its deployed harmonization pipeline uses explicit algorithms for deduplication, satellite-fleet consolidation, spatial and temporal matching, viewing-angle correction, and observation-effort normalization. It does not use a generative model to fabricate fire detections or a neural network to predict fires.

The application also includes synthetic demonstration fixtures generated by a deterministic, seeded script. These fixtures are clearly distinguished from NASA observations and from measured validation results. Their purpose is to demonstrate the interface when live data or the historical archive is unavailable. Dataset provenance and the synthetic designation are documented in `web/src/mocks/README.md` and `web/src/mocks/metadata.json`; this statement additionally discloses AI assistance in developing the generation code.

## Images, video, and audio

The globe background is derived from NASA Blue Marble imagery. Case-study charts are programmatically rendered from computed results. These assets are not presented as generative-AI images. The reviewed tracked submission contains no video or audio deliverable.

Any AI-generated image or video added to the final presentation must carry a visible AI-use watermark. Any AI-generated audio must identify that use in its accompanying description and metadata. Those requirements also apply to media produced separately from this repository.
